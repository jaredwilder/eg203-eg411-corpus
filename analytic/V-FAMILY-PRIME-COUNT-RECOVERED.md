## V-family prime-count lower bound

There exist effectively computable constants

\[
c>0,\qquad D_0\in\mathbb N
\]

such that for every ordinary `m` and every `D>=D_0`,

\[
\boxed{
\pi_V(m,D)\ge c\,\mathfrak S(m)\,D.
}
\]

One admissible pair recorded in the recovered paper is

\[
\boxed{c=0.25,\qquad D_0=10^{87}.}
\]

The paper source points to its kappa, Bombieri–Vinogradov/sieve, and singular-series sections for the proof.
